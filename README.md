# CastLiveNow

**Stream · Connect · Belong**

CastLiveNow is a live-streaming community where broadcasters and viewers can
come together, socialize, make friends, and go live. We believe broadcasters
matter, and want everyone to feel welcome and comfortable being part of the
community.

We are committed to making CastLiveNow a safe, positive place to connect.
Harassment and bullying have no place here, and we take bullying seriously.

Learn more at [castlivenow.com](https://castlivenow.com).
